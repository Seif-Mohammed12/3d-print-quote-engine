# Storefront (home, shop, product page)

Mode: Persuade (home), Operate-leaning commerce (shop, product). Mobile first, 375px.
Audience: young Egyptians from Instagram/Facebook on phones. Job: see the real object, pick colours, buy (COD/InstaPay, Aramex or pickup). Custom printing (STL quote, design help, bulk) is secondary.
User steer: dark, professional, aesthetic; no regional theming; nothing cartoonish. Chose "Label Catalogue", asked to tweak and polish it further.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every product is a release from a small independent label: catalogue number, square sleeve, one ink, nothing else. Refuses the centred hero + icon cards + neon-on-navy storefront.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite #0E0E0F ground, raised #1A1A1C, hairline rules #2A2A2D, paper-white #EDEAE4 text and solid primary buttons, warm grey #8C8A86 secondary, one matte orange ink #FF5B1F used flat and small. Host Grotesk only, tabular figures. Square sleeves, 1px ruled tables, 2px radius maximum. A coloured sleeve spine carries the chosen plastic colour and the catalogue number.

STORY: The visitor sees the actual object first, understands price, material, colours and ~48h printing at a glance, picks a colour, adds to cart in one thumb movement; then learns a person in New Cairo makes these, and that custom work is available.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: slim bar (wordmark left, Cart (n) right). Newest release as a full-width square sleeve with its spine on the left edge. Below: product name at ~44px, a ruled three-cell spec row (catalogue no. / material / printed in), price and a full-width paper-white Add to cart, all inside 844px. Desktop: sleeve left 7 columns, release text and action right 5 columns.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7 ("record-label catalogue"), assigned by the roll; raises: ruled tables as composition, colour previews before commit (spine), one object per viewport on phones, signed maker line with last-printed date, every control labelled in words. Signature interaction: choosing a colour wipes the spine to that colour. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
