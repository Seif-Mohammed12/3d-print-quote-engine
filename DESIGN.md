# Design System: 3D Print It

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Label Catalogue"**

Every product is a release from a small independent label: it gets a catalogue number, a square sleeve, and one ink. The storefront reads like a label's back catalogue laid out on a graphite board. The object comes first, in a square sleeve whose coloured spine carries the plastic colour the visitor picked. Price, material and the roughly 48-hour print time follow straight after, in ruled spec rows. The maker in New Cairo comes in further down the page, signed and dated.

The system is dark, flat and quiet so the prints carry the colour. The board is a near-black graphite, type is a warm paper-white, and one matte orange ink appears only in small, flat doses: catalogue numbers, active underlines, the cart count, focus rings and text selection. Composition comes from 1px ruled tables, not floating cards. Lists, spec sheets, terms of sale and the phone menu all sit as ruled rows, like a track list. Every control is labelled in words ("Menu", "Cart", "Close"), and every colour has a visible name.

This system replaces an earlier neon-on-navy, glass-card storefront. The user confirmed those devices as bans (see Do's and Don'ts).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Graphite board, paper-white type, one matte orange ink used flat and small.
- Host Grotesk only, tabular figures throughout, display set tight and large.
- Square sleeves with a coloured spine that carries the catalogue number.
- 1px ruled tables as the main compositional device; no card grids.
- Corners are square. 2px is the most rounding anything gets.
- Flat. Depth comes only from rules and a slightly raised graphite panel.
- One authored motion: the spine wipe when a colour is chosen.

## Colors

A graphite-and-paper monochrome, with one orange ink applied like a printed label number.

### Primary
- **Label Orange** (label-orange): the one ink. Catalogue numbers in rows and spec cells, the 2px active-nav underline and the active-filter bar, link hover underlines on product names, the filled cart count, the focus ring, the text caret and the selection highlight. It is always flat and always small. Fills, gradients and glows are out.

### Neutral
- **Paper White** (paper-white): all primary text, and the fill of the primary action button. A warm off-white, so the board never reads as pure black on pure white.
- **Graphite** (graphite): the page board, the nav bar, the phone buy bar, inputs and toasts.
- **Raised Graphite** (graphite-raised): the one raised surface. Panels, popovers, the commissions band on Home, photo placeholders and loading skeletons.
- **Lifted Graphite** (graphite-lift): fills selected colour chips and swatches, and the empty cart count.
- **Hover Graphite** (graphite-hover): hover surface for menu items and outline controls.
- **Hairline** (hairline): the default 1px rule between rows and around panels.
- **Rule** (rule): the stronger rule. Outlines on secondary buttons, inputs and unselected chips, the dashed edge of photo placeholders, link underlines at rest, and the scrollbar thumb.
- **Warm Grey** (warm-grey): secondary text. Spec labels, descriptions, inactive nav, shelf headings.

### Status
- **Signal Red** (signal-red): destructive actions and form errors only.
- **Print Green** (print-green): success states only.

### Named Rules
**The One Ink Rule.** Label Orange covers well under 5% of any screen. It marks numbers, active state and focus, never an area. No button is filled orange.

**The Paper Button Rule.** The primary action is a solid paper-white block with graphite text. On hover it brightens to pure white. Colour never carries the call to action.

**The Prints Bring the Colour Rule.** The only saturated colours beyond the ink are plastic colours: spines, swatches and photos. The interface stays graphite so a chosen filament reads true.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Host Grotesk (with Noto Sans Arabic, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Host Grotesk (with Noto Sans Arabic, system-ui, sans-serif)

**Character:** One grotesk across the whole system, from the 88px hero down to the 12px spine label. Weight, size and tight tracking set the hierarchy; a second family is never brought in for that. The body runs tabular figures with stylistic set 01 on (`"tnum" 1, "ss01" 1`), so prices and catalogue numbers line up like a spec sheet.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, 44px phone / 88px desktop, line-height 0.92, -0.04em): the featured release name in the Home hero. Used once per site.
- **Page title** (700, 40px phone / 72px desktop, 0.95): titles on Services, About and Not Found. The shelf pages (Shop, Gallery, Contact) step to 60px on desktop.
- **Product title** (700, 40px phone / 56px desktop, 0.95, -0.035em): the release name on the product page.
- **Section heading** (700, 36px phone / 56px desktop, line-height 1, -0.035em): the shared page/section heading component.
- **Headline** (700): 32px / 48px for the maker statement, 28px / 36px for section h2s ("The catalogue", "Materials"), 30px for service route names on desktop. Tracking -0.025 to -0.03em.
- **Menu link** (700, 26px, -0.02em): phone menu entries, set large like a track list in 60px ruled rows.
- **Title** (24px bold for sub-sections and dialog titles; 20px semibold for product names in the shop grid; 18px semibold for catalogue-row names and commission rows).
- **Wordmark** (800, 19px nav / 24px footer, -0.03em): "3D Print It", set in type with no logo mark.
- **Liner notes** (400, 17px, line-height 1.625, max 60 to 65ch): long-form product descriptions, the About story and contact details.
- **Body** (400, 16px, line-height 1.55, +0.005em): default running text.
- **UI** (15px): nav links, legends, form labels (semibold), quiet links, filter tabs, spec tables on the product page.
- **Small** (14px): spec labels, row descriptions, swatch names, footer column heads, legal line. The 28px bold price on the product page also sits in this tabular voice.
- **Count** (13px): the cart count badge.
- **Label** (600, 12px, +0.06em, vertical): the catalogue number running up the sleeve spine. This is the only positive tracking in the system.

### Named Rules
**The One Family Rule.** Host Grotesk only, weights 400 to 800. Noto Sans Arabic is there as a fallback for user-entered Arabic (names, addresses), which keeps its own direction through `dir="auto"`.

**The Tight Display Rule.** Headings track negative (-0.02em by default, down to -0.04em at display size), balance their line breaks, and sit at line-height 1 or below from 36px up.

**The Words Not Badges Rule.** Labels are sentence case in plain words. Uppercase tracked kickers and eyebrows above headings are not part of this system.

## Layout

A single frame, max 1240px wide, with a 16px gutter on phones and 32px from 768px up. Desktop compositions sit on a 12-column grid with 40px gaps. The Home hero puts the sleeve across 7 columns and the release text across 5, bottom-aligned. Section intros take 4 to 5 columns against 7 to 8 of content. On phones everything stacks to one column, with one object per viewport. The featured sleeve, name, three-cell spec row, colour chips and a full-width Add to cart all fit inside the first phone screen.

The spacing rhythm is ruled and vertical. Table cells are padded 12px, catalogue rows 16px top and bottom, commission rows 20px. Sections sit 64px apart on phones and 96 to 112px apart on desktop. The widest gap (112px phone / 160px desktop) is kept for the line above the maker section. The shop grid runs 1/2/3 columns with a 24px column gap and 48px row gap.

The nav is fixed at 56px. The shop's shelf filter sticks directly under it. On the phone product page a fixed 72px buy bar (price, chosen colour, Add to cart) sits in thumb reach, and the body reserves matching bottom padding plus the safe-area inset. Toasts lift 88px on phones to clear it.

### Named Rules
**The Ruled Table Rule.** Lists, specs, terms of sale, contact details and the phone menu are rows separated by 1px rules. A section heading sits on a full-strength paper-white rule. Groups of rows close with a hairline. Never use floating card grids for lists.

**The One Object Rule.** On a phone, one release fills the viewport at a time. Its name, price and action stay with it.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from two things only: 1px rules (hairline and rule) and one slightly raised graphite surface (graphite-raised) for panels, popovers, placeholders and the commissions band. Nothing floats, glows or blurs.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Toast** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -8px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`): the only shadow. It separates a transient toast from the board it overlaps.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Board Rule.** If something needs to stand apart, give it a rule or the raised graphite fill. Don't use a shadow, a blur or a glow.

## Shapes

Square by default. Buttons, panels, chips, swatches, inputs, sleeves, toasts and placeholders have square corners (0px). The 2px maximum appears only where library primitives pick up the shared radius. Borders are always 1px, except the 2px ink underline for active navigation and the 2px active-filter bar. Photo placeholders use a dashed 1px rule, the one dashed edge in the system, which marks an honest gap waiting on a real photo.

The signature silhouette is the square sleeve: a 1:1 frame with a vertical colour spine down its left edge (28px phone / 36px desktop). Catalogue-row thumbnails repeat it at 72px / 96px with a 6px spine of stacked colour bands.

### Named Rules
**The Square Sleeve Rule.** Product imagery is always 1:1, always with a spine on the left. Maker and studio photography is the one exception and runs at 4:3.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid, square and labelled in words.
- **Shape:** square corners (0px), minimum height 48px (44px in dense grid actions), 24px horizontal padding, 16px semibold (15px in grid actions).
- **Primary:** paper-white fill, graphite text. Price can ride inside the label ("Add to cart · 450 EGP").
- **Hover / Focus:** primary brightens to pure white over 200ms. Every focusable element gets a 2px Label Orange outline at 2px offset. Disabled drops to 50% opacity.
- **Secondary:** transparent with a 1px rule outline and paper-white text. On hover the outline turns paper-white.
- **Quiet link:** text with a 1px rule-coloured underline at 4px offset. On hover the underline turns paper-white.

### Chips (colour selection)
Every colour is named. Nothing hides behind a tooltip.
- **Swatch grid** (product page and quick add): 2 columns on phones, 3 from 640px. Each tile is 48px tall with a 24px colour square and the colour name.
- **Compact chips** (home hero): a single snap-scrolling row on phones that wraps on desktop. Each chip is 44px tall with a 20px colour square.
- **State:** unselected has a rule outline and warm-grey text. Hover moves to a warm-grey outline and paper-white text. Selected has a paper-white outline, lifted-graphite fill and paper-white text. The legend shows the chosen name on the right ("Colour ... Sunset Orange").

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** square (0px).
- **Background:** raised graphite for panels. Error and empty states are a bare 1px hairline box with 24px padding.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 20 to 24px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 1px rule outline, graphite fill, square corners, 48px tall, 12px horizontal padding, 16px text. The label sits above the field in 15px semibold, never as a placeholder alone.
- **Focus:** the outline turns paper-white, and the caret is Label Orange.
- **Error:** Signal Red text, with plain-language messages.

### Navigation
- **Bar:** fixed, 56px, graphite with a hairline bottom rule. Wordmark on the left. Text links on desktop (15px, warm grey, paper-white on hover; the active link is paper-white with a 2px orange underline at 10px offset). On the right: "Account"/"Log in", "Cart" with a square count (orange when it has items, lifted graphite when empty), and "Menu" on phones.
- **Phone menu:** a full-screen graphite sheet with the links as 26px bold ruled rows, 60px tall. It closes with a worded "Close" control.
- **Footer:** hairline top rule, wordmark, contact links as quiet links, three link columns under small warm-grey heads.

### Sleeve (signature)
A square record sleeve. The product photo fills the right side, and the colour spine runs down the left edge with the catalogue number written vertically from bottom to top in 12px semibold. Spine text flips between graphite and paper-white based on the spine colour's luminance. On the product page and the Home hero the spine shows the single chosen colour. When a new colour is chosen, the new colour wipes down over the old one (`spine-wipe`, 520ms, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). On listings the spine shows every available colour as stacked bands.

### Catalogue Row (signature)
One release per ruled row. On desktop: catalogue number in orange, a 96px sleeve thumbnail, name and one-line description, colour count, price. On phones: a 72px thumbnail, then the number above the name, with the price on the right. Hovering the name brings in a 2px orange underline. Discounted prices show the old price struck through in small warm grey.

### Photo Pending (signature)
An honest blank plate in place of a missing photo. It has a dashed 1px rule edge, a raised graphite fill, a small line camera icon, "Photo on the way" and the shot description taken from the shot list. Placeholders are never filled with stock or generated imagery.

### Phone Buy Bar
Fixed to the bottom of the product page on phones only: price, chosen colour and a primary Add to cart, above a hairline top rule. It respects the safe-area inset.

### Named Rules
**The Spine Rule.** The sleeve spine is the only moving part. Choosing a colour wipes it; nothing else animates on its own. Under reduced motion, animations and transitions collapse to instant. Content never fades or slides up on scroll.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep Label Orange to numbers, active underlines, the cart count, focus, caret and selection.
- **Do** make the primary action a solid paper-white button, at least 48px tall, labelled in words. Put the price in the label when it helps.
- **Do** set lists, specs and terms as 1px ruled rows, and open sections with a heading on a paper-white rule.
- **Do** show product imagery in a square sleeve with a left colour spine carrying the catalogue number.
- **Do** name every colour visibly next to its swatch.
- **Do** keep every touch target at least 44px, and keep the phone buy bar and toast offsets clear of each other.
- **Do** use the dashed Photo Pending plate, with its shot-list description, wherever a real photo is missing.
- **Do** respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. The spine wipe is the one authored animation (520ms, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).

### Don't:
- **Don't** round corners past 2px, and don't use pill shapes.
- **Don't** add shadows beyond the toast, or blur or translucent surfaces.
- **Don't** set uppercase, letter-spaced kickers or eyebrows above headings.
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface, or switch off tabular figures for prices.
- **Don't** fade or slide content in on scroll.
- **Don't** fill large areas or buttons with Label Orange.
- **Don't** bring back the replaced system: no gradient text, no glass, no neon glow, no centred hero with two buttons, no icon-tile card grids.
